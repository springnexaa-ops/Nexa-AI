# NEXA Post-Market Surveillance Baseline

Track, review and trend:
- clinical complaints and user-reported failures;
- safety incidents and near misses;
- hallucination or unsupported-citation events;
- evidence freshness/retrieval failures;
- model/provider outages and material model changes;
- cybersecurity vulnerabilities and incidents;
- subgroup performance and drift where measurable;
- clinician override, modification and rejection rates;
- corrective and preventive actions (CAPA).

## Escalation
Define severity, reporting timelines, ownership, containment, investigation, root-cause analysis, corrective action, effectiveness verification and closure criteria before clinical deployment.

## Change control
Material changes to model, prompt policy, retrieval corpus, clinical workflow, intended use or software dependencies should trigger a documented impact assessment and, where applicable, regression testing and re-validation.
