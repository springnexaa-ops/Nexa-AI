# NEXA Medical Intended Use and Claims Control

## Current engineering position
NEXA medical mode is intended for clinician-supervised medical education and decision support, including structured interpretation of supplied electrodiagnostic/clinical information and retrieval of authoritative medical evidence.

It is not intended to replace a qualified clinician, independently establish a definitive diagnosis, prescribe treatment, or represent model output as a medical-device regulatory authorization.

## Claims-control rules
- Every externally visible clinical claim must map to an intended-use statement or validated feature.
- Claims must identify the evidence basis and limitations.
- New clinical capabilities require documented risk assessment and validation before production claims are expanded.
- Marketing language must not exceed the validated intended use.

## Release gate
A medical feature is not considered inspection-ready merely because code works. Release requires documented verification, validation evidence, risk review, cybersecurity review, privacy review and approval through the project's quality process.
